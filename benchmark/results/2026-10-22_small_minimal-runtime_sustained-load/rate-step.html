How long each rate step runs

Each offered rate is one cell. Arrivals run for the warm-up plus the measured duration: 5 s + 25 s for the agent-sandbox sweeps, and 15 s + 60 s for SandboxManager. Calls still in flight then finish (the drain; nothing is cut off). The warm pool is reset before the next rate starts (the cooldown). Below saturation the drain is about a second. Once an arm falls behind, the drain can outlast the arrivals.

  • Measured windows include the drain. Delivered calls/s divides completed calls by the time from the first measured call to the last release, so at saturating rates a 25 s cell reports a 48–66 s window.
  • The long SandboxManager steps are cooldown. After the 3 and 4 calls/s cells, the harness waited 16–17 minutes for that rate's sandbox pods to finish terminating before starting the next rate.
  • A 30 s step is about as long as a 50-sandbox pool lasts. In longer runs (next panel) pool 50 was empty after ~32 s at 3 calls/s and ~27 s at 4 calls/s, because refills start slowly and average only ~2.5/s under churn. A 30 s step ends right around that point, so the sweep shows the pool at its best. At 3 calls/s the refills nearly keep up even once it's empty (94% of calls still pooled over 150 s); at 4 calls/s they don't (74%, acquire p95 17 s).
