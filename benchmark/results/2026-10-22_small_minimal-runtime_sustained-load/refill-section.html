Refill rate (10 s bins)

  • Refill is fast until IPs run out. On a quiet cluster the pool fills at ~13 sandboxes/s, and one 50-call burst is replaced within seconds. Under sustained churn it averages ~2.5/s, with dips every ~30 s: each deleted pod's IP is held 30 s before reuse, so new pods wait in Pending for one.
  • The pool empties fast, then runs near zero. At 3 calls/s it was empty by 32 s, but refills nearly match the arrival rate, so most calls still got a pooled sandbox. At 4 calls/s the gap is too wide and acquires fall back to cold starts.
  • Recovery takes ~30 s after the load stops: the same IP hold, playing out once more.
