Reading it

  • With a runtime that starts in milliseconds, agent-sandbox cold-start acquire fell to 0.44 s p50 at one worker (5.62 s with the Python runtime), now below SandboxManager's 1.05 s.
  • Throughput is unchanged at ~2–2.5 calls/s for every arm that creates sandboxes: the cluster's pod-IP turnover still sets the ceiling.
  • A 32-call burst against a pool of 32 ran at 18.31 calls/s (13.45 with the Python runtime); with no pool, 1.86 calls/s.
  • At 16 and 32 workers the stop rule ended cells early when a call waited over 60 s for a pod IP; their numbers cover the calls up to that point.
